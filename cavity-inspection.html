Joint commandsprojected onto the null space of the EE position constraint

Hold the − and + buttons, or the keys Q/A, W/S, E/D, R/F, T/G and Y/H, to move the joints q₁ to q₆. Press Report or Space when a damaged part is inside the circle of the camera image. Drag the external view to rotate it. P pauses.

  • Disconnected connectorThe plug lies out of its socket and its pins are exposed.
  • Damaged cableThe insulation is torn: copper conductor and loose strands.
  • Cracked bracketThe upright is cracked and bent; a fragment lies next to it.
  • Burnt terminalSoot around one terminal and a charred conductor.

Intact parts of the same four kinds are installed elsewhere in the housing. The arm, the soft extension and the controller are those of the simulations of the paper: a UR10 with a Cosserat-rod extension and two elastic tendons, whose spans set the tensions that bend the extension. A joint command u is applied as q̇ = N_c u + J_c⁺ k_c e_c, with the null-space projector N_c = I − J_c⁺ J_c of the EE position constraint, and the motion stops at the limits of the span domain identified from probing motions and at any contact. The solution runs the controller of the paper from the start configuration: the damped least-squares law in the null space of the constraint and a quadratic program with control barrier functions for the walls, the contents of the housing and the span domain drive the tip to one viewpoint per damaged part. A report is accepted when the damaged part lies within 13° of the optical axis, closer than 24 cm and in line of sight.
