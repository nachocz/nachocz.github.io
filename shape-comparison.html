Shape control requires a numerical error between the current and target shapes. This game uses the Dice overlap between a clay block and each target. Remove or restore material until the score reaches 95 %. Complete all five orders; the clock records the total time.

Drag on the block to apply the tool. Keyboard: arrow keys move the tool, Space applies it, T switches between removing and restoring, [ and ] change its size, and P pauses.

  • Excess materialClay outside the target shape, marked red in the order.
  • Missing materialTarget area without clay, marked blue in the order.
  • Order completeAt 95 % the piece moves to its display case and the next order arrives.

The score is the Dice overlap 2|B ∩ T| / (|B| + |T|) between the clay particles B and the particles inside the target T. Particles within 0.6 spacings of the target contour are excluded, so the score does not depend on where the contour intersects a particle. A shape controller can use this measure as feedback: after each robot motion, it measures the shape and commands the next motion to reduce the difference.
