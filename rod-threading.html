Rod threading

Shape control computes the robot motions that bring a deformable object to a target shape, as in cable routing, food handling or surgery. Here two robots hold an elastic rod in a vertical plane, and panels with a gap shaped like a reachable rod configuration move toward it: deform the rod so that it passes through each gap. Show solution runs the shape-servoing loop of our tutorial, which estimates how the rod responds to the grippers and corrects that estimate as it moves.

Right robot joints

Drag a gripper to move it, drag the ring above it to rotate it, or drag the rod to move both grippers. Keyboard: arrow keys move the selected gripper, Q and E rotate it, Space switches gripper, P pauses.

  • Clean passThe whole rod is inside the gap: +100.
  • Close passAt least 75% of the rod is inside the gap: +40.
  • CollisionLess than 75% of the rod is inside the gap: one life lost.

Each gap is the equilibrium shape of the same rod for a reachable pair of gripper poses, widened by the current clearance, so every gap can be matched exactly. After every pass the clearance decreases and the panels move faster.
