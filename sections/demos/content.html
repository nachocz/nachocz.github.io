Demos

Interactive research demos

  • A Tutorial on Shape Servoing of Deformable Objects Data-driven shape control of an elastic rod held by two robot arms.
  • Ergodic Heat Coverage Control Model-free regulation of surface temperature with a robot-mounted heat source. I. Cuiral-Zueco, J. Thomas and Y. Mezouar, IEEE Transactions on Robotics, 2026.
  • Passive Soft Extensions Actuated by Manipulator Motion A tendon-driven soft extension bent and controlled through the joint motion of the arm.
  • Time Consistent Surface Mapping for Deformable Object Shape Control Time-consistent functional maps between the mesh perceived at every frame and the target shape, used as the reference for 3D shape control. I. Cuiral-Zueco and G. López-Nicolás, IEEE Transactions on Automation Science and Engineering, 2025.
  • Multiscale Procrustes-Based 3-D Shape Control 3D shape control of a deformable object with 6-DoF gripper actions computed by Procrustes analysis at multiple scales. I. Cuiral-Zueco and G. López-Nicolás, IEEE/ASME Transactions on Mechatronics, 2024.
  • Stereo Visual SLAM for Medical Endoscopy Stereo ORB-SLAM2 evaluated on laparoscopy sequences and extended with a dense tissue map built from stereo disparities.

Mini-games

  • Shape similarity Easy Remove or restore material until a block matches each target.
  • Rod threading Medium Deform a rod held by two robots so that it passes through approaching gaps.
  • Dino egg incubator Medium Keep a dinosaur egg uniformly warm with a moving heat source until it hatches. I. Cuiral-Zueco, J. Thomas and Y. Mezouar, IEEE Transactions on Robotics, 2026.
  • Cavity inspection Hard Find damaged parts inside a closed housing with the tip camera of a soft extension moved in the null space of the arm.
