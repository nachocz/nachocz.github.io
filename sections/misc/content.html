People I admire

These are some people whose work I admire and often come back to.

  • 3Blue1Brown

    Grant Sanderson is a genius at explaining maths. His animated series on linear algebra, calculus and neural networks are worth watching more than once.

  • Veritasium

    Derek Muller and his team make some of the best science and engineering videos on YouTube, often built around an experiment or a common misconception.

  • braintruffle

    Torsten Heinze is a former nonlinear dynamics researcher and, frankly, a genius. His videos on fluids, chaos and control take 1,000 to 3,000 hours each, simulations included.

  • Zach Star

    Clever and often very funny maths and engineering videos.

  • Lemnismath

    One of the best maths channels in Spanish. Jaime covers paradoxes, fractals and the history of mathematics, and is building a computer out of marbles and wood.

  • Vulfpeck

    Minimalist funk band from Ann Arbor. Start with Dean Town and that Joe Dart bass line.

  • Snarky Puppy · Lingus

    Ten minutes from the album We Like It Here (2014), with Cory Henry’s famous keyboard solo towards the end.

  • David Bull · Slow woodblock printing

    An hour and a half of a master printmaker at work, one sheet after another, without cuts. Good for slowing down.

Educational robotics: LoCo CORP

I co-founded LoCo CORP, an initiative to update technology education in primary and secondary schools. We developed the Noctis and Aurel educational robots, together with workshops and courses based on them. In the workshops the robot was treated as a technical system rather than a toy: students worked on its electronics, programming and hardware design, with the aim of understanding the technology they use rather than only consuming it.

My main contribution was the LoCoVerso, a fictional setting in which the robots are newly discovered “species” on a remote planet. The narrative presented the technical lessons as scientific fieldwork, and students designed and customised new species (robots), combining the technical content with creative and artistic work.
