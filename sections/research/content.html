Research

I work on robotic manipulation along two lines: shape control of deformable objects and thermal servoing. In both, the robot motion is computed in closed loop from sensor measurements of the object (its shape or its surface temperature), with little or no model of the material.

Deformable object manipulation

Shape control: estimating the shape of an object from sensor data, establishing correspondences between its deformed configurations, and computing the robot motions that drive it to a target shape. I focus on robust, data-efficient methods and on coordinated manipulation with several robots.

Thermal servoing

Regulation of the temperature distribution of a surface with a robot-mounted heat source, as required in adhesive activation, composite curing or coating drying. Ergodic heat coverage control (EHCC) determines where to heat, by matching the time-averaged heat footprint of the source to the heat deficit of the surface, and how much, by adjusting the source-to-surface distance, without a thermal model of the material.

Topics
Shape control, deformable-object manipulation, thermal servoing, robot perception and visual servoing.
Methods
Perception-to-manipulation pipelines, shape analysis, continuum mechanics, non-linear and ergodic control, and data-driven estimation and control.
Applications
Robotic manipulation, cooperative robotics and manufacturing, including the thermal processing of materials.
